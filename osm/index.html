Surfaces and stairs, from OpenStreetMap

Published 8 October 2026

Peakline carries a database of paths' surfaces and flights of stairs, for the strips under a route's elevation chart. It is derived from OpenStreetMap, and you may have it here, free, under the Open Database License.

Download

  • surfaces.geojson.gz (4.7 MB, 30 MB unpacked): the lines as GeoJSON in WGS84, each with its surface (or null) and whether it is stairs.
  • surfaces.hk3a (2.1 MB): the same lines exactly as the app carries them.
  • SHA256SUMS

What it holds

From Geofabrik's Hong Kong extract of OpenStreetMap, of 5 October 2026:

  • every way whose surface tag names a known surface, as one of ten: asphalt, concrete, paving, gravel, dirt, grass, sand, wood, paved, unpaved;
  • every way tagged highway=steps, marked as stairs;
  • the ways of no known surface within 24 m of those;
  • each line simplified to 1 m, its points in the Hong Kong 1980 Grid (EPSG:2326) in steps of 0.5 m. The GeoJSON gives them in WGS84 to seven decimal places.

135,769 lines: 57,266 of a known surface or stairs and 78,503 of no known surface, with 595,867 points.

How it was made

With the tools in Peakline's engine:

sh docs/verification/scripts/fetch_osm_ways.sh data/sources/osm/2026-10-06
hk3d-bake surfaces <dir> --ways data/sources/osm/2026-10-06/ways.geojson
hk3d-bake pack --levels 5-5 --out surfaces.hk3a <dir>
hk3d-bake surfaces-geojson surfaces.hk3a --out surfaces.geojson

Each way's surface is mapped to the nearest of the ten above. Ways without one are kept only near a way with one, or near stairs. Nothing is added that OpenStreetMap does not hold.

The .hk3a format

An archive of cells of 24 × 19.2 km. Each cell is a list of lines, a line written whole into every cell it crosses. A line has a kind byte, its surface in the low seven bits and stairs in the top bit, followed by its points as zigzag variable-length steps of 0.5 m. The GeoJSON holds the same lines, each once.

內容

取自 Geofabrik 於 2026 年 10 月 5 日的 OpenStreetMap 香港數據：

  • 所有 surface 標籤屬已知路面的路徑，歸入十類之一：瀝青、混凝土、鋪砌、碎石、泥地、草地、沙地、木、已鋪設、未鋪設；
  • 所有標記為 highway=steps 的路徑，列為樓梯；
  • 上述路徑 24 米範圍內未標路面的路徑；
  • 每條線簡化至 1 米，坐標為香港 1980 方格網（EPSG:2326），以 0.5 米為單位；GeoJSON 則以 WGS84 表示，至小數點後七位。

共 135,769 條線：57,266 條屬已知路面或樓梯，78,503 條未知路面，合共 595,867 個點。

製作方法

使用 Peakline 引擎的工具，指令見英文版。每條路徑的 surface 會歸入上述十類中最接近的一類；未標路面的路徑只在接近已知路面或樓梯時保留。不會加入任何 OpenStreetMap 沒有的內容。
