Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Peakline collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

Peakline is a 3D map of Hong Kong for iPhone, made by an independent developer in Hong Kong ("we"). This policy explains what the app and this website do with information.

Your location

If you allow it, Peakline uses your location to show where you are on the map and to move the map there. It is used on your iPhone only, while you use the app. It is not stored, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. Peakline asks for it only when you tap the location button, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Routes and GPX files

Routes you add, the GPX files you open with Peakline, and the checkpoints you add to them are kept in the app on your iPhone. They leave your device only when you share or export them yourself.

Photos

If you save a video you made to your photo library, Peakline asks for permission to add to it. It cannot see or read your photos.

Videos you make

Videos made during the free trial carry a visible Peakline watermark. Every video carries an invisible mark that records the date it was made and a random number, and ends with credits for the map's data. Neither contains any information about you or your device.

Purchases

Purchases are made and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID, email address or payment details.

  • The app reads what you have bought from Apple, on your iPhone, to unlock it.
  • Each purchase carries a random identifier made on your iPhone. It is not linked to your identity, and it lets purchases be recognised if accounts are ever added to Peakline.
  • How many free trial videos you have made is kept on your iPhone, in its Keychain, and is never sent anywhere.

Map downloads

The detailed map of all Hong Kong is downloaded from Apple's servers, which host it for Peakline. Apple's privacy policy applies to that download. We receive nothing from it.

What we receive

Peakline runs no servers of its own and sends nothing to us. The only connections it makes are to Apple's services: the App Store, purchases and map downloads.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and app usage statistics, under Apple's privacy policy. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. It remembers the language you choose in your browser's storage, on your device only. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. If you contact support through GitHub, what you write is public on GitHub and handled under GitHub's terms.

Children

Peakline does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact support.

私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日

Peakline 不會收集任何個人資料。沒有帳戶、沒有廣告、沒有分析工具，亦沒有追蹤。你在 App 內的一切都只留在你的 iPhone 上。

Peakline 是一個供 iPhone 使用的香港 3D 地圖，由一位香港獨立開發者（「我們」）開發。本政策說明 App 及本網站如何處理資料。

你的位置

如你允許，Peakline 會使用你的位置，在地圖上顯示你身處何處，並將地圖移到該處。位置只會在你使用 App 時、在你的 iPhone 上使用，不會被儲存，亦不會傳送給我們或任何人。Peakline 只會在你按下定位按鈕時才要求權限；你可隨時在「設定」›「私隱與保安」›「定位服務」中關閉。

路線及 GPX 檔案

你加入的路線、用 Peakline 開啟的 GPX 檔案，以及你加入的檢查站，都儲存在你 iPhone 上的 App 內。只有在你自行分享或匯出時，它們才會離開你的裝置。

相片

如你將製作的影片儲存到相片圖庫，Peakline 會要求加入相片的權限。它無法查看或讀取你的相片。

你製作的影片

免費試用期間製作的影片會帶有可見的 Peakline 水印。每段影片都帶有一個隱形標記，記錄影片的製作日期及一個隨機數字，並在結尾列出地圖資料來源。兩者都不包含任何關於你或你裝置的資料。

購買

所有購買均由 Apple 透過 App Store 進行及處理。我們永遠不會收到你的姓名、Apple ID、電郵地址或付款資料。

  • App 會在你的 iPhone 上向 Apple 讀取你已購買的項目，以便解鎖。
  • 每次購買會附上一個在你 iPhone 上產生的隨機識別碼。它與你的身分無關，用途是日後 Peakline 如加入帳戶功能時，可以辨認已有的購買。
  • 你已使用的免費試用影片數目，儲存在你 iPhone 的「鑰匙圈」內，不會傳送到任何地方。

地圖下載

全港詳細地圖由 Apple 的伺服器代 Peakline 託管及下載，該下載受 Apple 的私隱政策規管。我們不會從中收到任何資料。

我們會收到甚麼

Peakline 沒有自己的伺服器，亦不會傳送任何資料給我們。它只會連接 Apple 的服務：App Store、購買及地圖下載。

如你在 iPhone 設定中選擇與 App 開發者分享分析資料，Apple 或會按其私隱政策，向我們提供匿名的當機報告及 App 使用統計。你可在「設定」›「私隱與保安」›「分析與改進」中更改。

本網站

本網站不使用 cookie、分析工具或任何第三方程式碼，只會在你的瀏覽器儲存空間（只在你的裝置上）記住你選擇的語言。本網站由 GitHub Pages 託管；GitHub 或會為保安理由記錄訪客的 IP 位址，受 GitHub 一般私隱聲明規管。如你經 GitHub 聯絡支援，你所寫的內容會在 GitHub 上公開，並受 GitHub 的條款規管。

兒童

Peakline 不會向任何人收集個人資料，包括兒童。

政策更改

如本政策有所更改，新版本連同日期會刊登在本頁。
